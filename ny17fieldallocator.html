The strategic read

Davidson's edge is Rockland, where the county party is behind her and she's the local name. The job there isn't persuasion — it's turnout. Bank every favorable vote.

Westchester is the largest, bluest county and the most contested — the county party stayed neutral. This is where doors change minds, not just turn them out. It's the battleground.

Putnam & Dutchess are smaller and tougher, but hold real high-turnout pockets (Philipstown, Beacon) worth a targeted visit — not a blanket one.

How it works — and what makes it sharp

This runs on public structural data today. The moment it's pointed at the campaign's own voter file, the municipality scores become precinct-level and the projections become real targets.

Data in the engine now

Real — county vote shares & electorate weights: 2024 NY-17 certified results by county; Rockland ≈ 40% of the Dem primary electorate.

Real — race structure: 5-way June 23 Dem primary, Cook/Inside Elections toss-up rating, latest public polling margin.

Modeled — municipality registrants, primary-turnout propensity, Davidson lean: illustrative placeholders, structured to match known county-level patterns.

What it becomes with the voter file

Swap the modeled layer for: NY State BOE enrollment by election district, VAN/PDI vote history (who actually votes in June primaries), and the campaign's own ID/canvass results.

Municipality rows become election-district rows. "Davidson lean" becomes real support ID. "Projected votes" becomes a turf cut a field director can print and walk. Same engine, live targets.

The priority score, in plain terms

For each place: expected primary voters = registered Dems × primary-turnout propensity (low-turnout June electorate, not the November universe). That prize is then weighted by how much a door knock moves it — highest where the race is close to 50/50 (persuasion), solid where there's a favorable base to turn out (GOTV), and discounted where the lean is unwinnable. Bank / Persuade / Hold falls out of that weighting. No black box — every number on the Priority Board is one multiplication you can defend to a county chair.

Open questions for the field team
